How I work

Principles I bring to every team.

  • Clarity before speed

    Goals, ownership and trade-offs are written down, so the team can move fast without waiting on me.

  • Grow people, not just systems

    Weekly 1:1s, honest feedback and stretch work that is real, not invented. A stronger team is the result I care about most.

  • Stay close to the work

    I still review architecture and design decisions, so what we decide is grounded in how the system actually behaves in production.

  • Steady through change

    Priorities, plans and teams shift. My job is to keep people focused and commitments realistic when they do.

  • Own outcomes, not tickets

    I work with product, design and business stakeholders on the whole customer journey, not only the backend behind it.

  • AI where it earns its place

    In the product and in our workflow, judged by what it actually improves, not by the hype.
